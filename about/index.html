About Me

Professional Summary

I am a software architect based in Tübingen, Germany, with a background in computer science (PhD, Summa Cum Laude) and over seven years of experience building embedded resimulation environments that run at scale in cloud-native verification and validation (V&V) platforms for automated driving (AD) at Robert Bosch GmbH. I lead cross-functional agile teams on an organizational and technical level to deliver high quality and AD-certified (ISO-26262) tools for safety-critical automotive software.

My approach emphasizes:

  • Reproducibility — deterministic executions, containerized environments, infrastructure as code
  • Scalable architecture — designing systems that grow from prototype to production across heterogeneous environments
  • Tooling mastery — deep customization of development environments for maximum productivity
  • Continuous integration — automated testing and deployment pipelines from day one
  • Cross-domain expertise — bridging scientific computing, cloud infrastructure, and embedded systems

What I Bring to a Team

  • Production experience architecting large-scale C++ and Python systems
  • Proven track record coordinating multiple agile teams as a technical lead
  • Fluency in Linux systems engineering and DevOps practices (Github, Kubernetes, Docker, Airflow)
  • Ability to bridge the gap between research/prototyping and production deployment
  • Strong emphasis on developer experience and team productivity tooling
  • Multilingual communication (German, English, Italian, Spanish)
