Sphinx and Esbonio

To setup esbonio in your actual project, use this snippet as a template for your .dir-local.el file 

((rst-mode
  . ((eglot-workspace-configuration
      . (:sphinx
         (:builderName "html"
                       :buildDir "./build"
                       :confDir "./source")
         :server
         (:logLevel "debug"))))))
