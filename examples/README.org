** Sphinx and Esbonio

To setup esbonio in your actual project, use this snippet as a
template for your =.dir-local.el= file

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
  ((rst-mode
    . ((eglot-workspace-configuration
        . (:sphinx
           (:builderName "html"
                         :buildDir "./build"
                         :confDir "./source")
           :server
           (:logLevel "debug"))))))
#+end_src
