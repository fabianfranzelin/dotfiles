* Professional Summary

I am a software architect based in Tübingen, Germany, with a
background in computer science (PhD, Summa Cum Laude) and over six
years of experience building cloud-native test and validation
platforms for autonomous driving at Robert Bosch GmbH. I lead
cross-functional agile teams to deliver scalable data pipelines,
resimulation environments, and statistical analysis tooling for
safety-critical automotive software.

My approach emphasizes:

- *Scalable architecture* — designing systems that grow from prototype
  to production across heterogeneous environments
- *Reproducibility* — deterministic builds, containerized environments,
  infrastructure as code
- *Tooling mastery* — deep customization of development environments for
  maximum productivity
- *Continuous integration* — automated testing and deployment pipelines
  from day one
- *Cross-domain expertise* — bridging scientific computing, cloud
  infrastructure, and embedded systems

* What I Bring to a Team

- Production experience architecting large-scale C++ and Python
  systems in the cloud
- Proven track record coordinating multiple agile teams as a technical
  lead
- Fluency in Linux systems engineering and DevOps practices
  (Kubernetes, Docker, Airflow)
- Ability to bridge the gap between research/prototyping and
  production deployment
- Strong emphasis on developer experience and team productivity
  tooling
- Multilingual communication (German, English, Italian, Spanish)
