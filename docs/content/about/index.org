* Professional Summary

I am a software architect based in Tübingen, Germany, with a
background in computer science (PhD, Summa Cum Laude) and over seven
years of experience building embedded resimulation environments that
run at scale in cloud-native verification and validation (V&V)
platforms for automated driving (AD) at Robert Bosch GmbH. I lead
cross-functional agile teams on an organizational and technical level
to deliver high quality and AD-certified (ISO-26262) tools for
safety-critical automotive software.

My approach emphasizes:

- *Reproducibility* — deterministic executions, containerized
  environments, infrastructure as code
- *Scalable architecture* — designing systems that grow from prototype
  to production across heterogeneous environments
- *Tooling mastery* — deep customization of development environments for
  maximum productivity
- *Continuous integration* — automated testing and deployment pipelines
  from day one
- *Cross-domain expertise* — bridging scientific computing, cloud
  infrastructure, and embedded systems

* What I Bring to a Team
